Limitations, in plain words

  • All results use synthetic or public data. They show that the pipeline is sound, not that the model is accurate on ShopUp merchants.
  • The delivery (REDX) signal is a hypothesis tested by ablation, including a zero-signal control. It is not an established predictor of repayment.
  • Labels exist only for merchants who previously received credit. The model may not generalize to applicants who would have been declined under earlier policy.
  • Scores are less certain for merchants with short history. A thin file is never treated as a bad file.
  • Group columns are used only to audit results, never as model inputs.
  • The tool supports a human decision. It must not be used for automatic approval or decline.
