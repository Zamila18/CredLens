# CredLens: design brief for the new interface

> **Changed on Oct 31, 2026 (team decision):** CredLens now also has a public landing page (`/`) and a "Choose a demo role" page (`/demo`). The demo-role page is not a login (no password, no identity check), and there is still no sign-up, "Apply", pricing or loan offer anywhere. The staff workspace moved to `/app`.

This file is the complete context for designing the CredLens web interface. Read all of it before drawing anything. Everything about the **look** (colours, fonts, layout style, illustrations, icons, spacing) is your choice. Everything about the **content** (pages, fields, words, numbers, rules) is fixed by this file: do not add features, buttons, fields or claims that are not here, and do not leave out the ones that are.

If something you want to draw is not covered here, leave it out rather than invent it.

---

## 1. What CredLens is

- A **staff dashboard for a lender** in Bangladesh. The example partner is ShopUp, which runs three services:
  - **Mokam**: wholesale ordering. A shop **buys stock** through it. (These are the shop's purchases, never its sales.)
  - **REDX**: parcel delivery. A shop **sends parcels** to its customers through it.
  - **Baki**: shop credit. A shop **borrows and repays** through it.
- Small shops often have no bank history ("thin files"). CredLens joins their purchases, deliveries and repayments into one view and gives a **readiness score** with plain-language reasons. A **credit officer reads it and decides**. The software never approves or declines anyone.
- It is a **prototype for a hackathon** (2026 Data, AI & Policy APAC Hackathon, theme "Financial Health Frontiers"), built by **Team 4Bytes**. All data is **synthetic demo data** (generated by the team, shaped by Bangladesh figures) or **public research data** (Taiwan, India, Home Credit, Kiva). No real customer appears anywhere.
- It is **not a consumer website**. Shop owners do not use it (except the one demo phone screen of the consent request, page 08). There is no "apply", "get a loan", "check your eligibility", pricing, sign-up or login.

The design should feel like a **professional, trustworthy fintech product for lending staff in Bangladesh**: calm, organised, humane, easy to scan, respectful of the shop owners it describes.

## 2. Who uses it (demo roles)

There is **no login**. A "demo role switch" picks one of four demo users. It must always be visible with the note **"A demo role switch, not a login."**

| Demo user | Role | May do |
|---|---|---|
| Officer 1, Officer 2 | officer | Everything except the audit log and data import. The only role that records decisions and questionnaire answers. |
| Admin | admin | Read everything, the audit log, data import. Cannot record decisions. |
| Viewer | viewer | Read only. Cannot record anything. |

Pages a role may not open are hidden from its navigation (Audit log and Data import show only for Admin). On the merchant page, Admin and Viewer see a note instead of the decision form: "Only officers record decisions. You are signed in as viewer-1, so this page is read-only. Switch the demo role to an officer to record one."

## 3. Words you need

| Term | Meaning on screen |
|---|---|
| Merchant | A small shop, shown by an ID like **M000031**, with district, town tier and shop type. Never a person's name. |
| Readiness score | A number from 0 to 100 = the calibrated probability of repaying in time × 100. |
| Coverage | How much of the evidence the source could give is present (0–100%). **Not** the score. A separate measure next to it. |
| Band | **Strong** (score 90 or more, enough coverage), **Review** (under 90), **Needs more info** (coverage under 45%, whatever the score). |
| Low-score warning | Score under 70: shown as "Review · low score" and a line "Low score: read every reason before deciding." |
| Reasons | Plain sentences that raised ("Helps") or lowered ("Holds back") the score, each with points (e.g. +1.66) and a number for context. |
| Summary | A short paragraph at the top of the score card describing the score. It never recommends lending or not. |
| Consent | The shop's permission to use its data. **No valid consent = no score, no decision, no questionnaire.** |
| Questionnaire | 10 optional financial-habit questions (answers 1–5). Shown **beside** the score, **never part of it** ("Directional · not part of the score"). |
| Decision | What the officer records: one of four actions, with a written reason for an override. **Append-only**: never edited or deleted. |
| Audit log | A record of who viewed or changed what, when. |
| Model card | A page explaining what the model is for, how it was tested and where it falls short, one per data source. |
| Data source / data mode | Which data the dashboard runs on: **synthetic_bd** (the demo) or the four public sources. |

## 4. Rules every screen must follow

1. **Banner on every page**: a thin strip naming the data source and ending with "Decision support only." For the demo: **"Synthetic demo data. Decision support only."** (Bangla: "সিনথেটিক ডেমো ডেটা। শুধু সিদ্ধান্তে সহায়তা।")
2. **A score never stands alone**: wherever a score appears, its coverage and band appear with it.
3. **"Needs more info" looks neutral**, never red or like a failure: it means too little data, not a bad shop. A thin file must never look worse than a low score.
4. **No Approve / Decline / Reject buttons anywhere.** The officer's four actions are exactly: **Accept suggestion · Override: raise · Override: lower · Needs more info.** An override requires a written reason (the button stays disabled until one is typed).
5. **Reasons are sentences**, never technical names.
6. **Language switch "English / বাংলা"** on every screen: it switches the whole interface. Numbers stay in English digits in both languages. Use a font that renders Bangla well (for example Noto Sans Bengali or Hind Siliguri, paired with a Latin sans-serif).
7. **Demo role switch** on every screen (see section 2).
8. **Colour is never the only signal**: bands and actions always have their text label too.
9. **Do not write**: "approved", "declined", "eligible", "rejected", "fair", "proven", "AI decides", "guaranteed".
10. **Public data sources show totals only.** In a public data mode there is no review queue and no merchant page. Home Credit rows are never shown at all.

Sentences the product uses and you may reuse: "The score suggests. You decide, and the record keeps why." · "Observations, not causes." · "A thin file shows as Needs more info, never as a low band." · "This supports a decision; the decision is yours." · "Decisions are append-only. To change course, record a new decision."

## 9. Do not design

- Approve, Decline, Reject, "Eligible", "Pre-approved", loan offers, interest rates, "Apply", sign-up or login pages.
- People's names or photos as merchants (merchants are IDs; an illustration of a generic shop in the hero is fine).
- Charts or numbers not listed in this file.
- A red or alarming "Needs more info".
- A score without its coverage and band.
- Any claim that the model is fair, proven, or works on real merchants.
